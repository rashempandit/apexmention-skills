# ApexMention

Five skills for getting a business named in AI answers: an AI visibility baseline, best-of list outreach, a ChatGPT business profile, Reddit and Quora answers that disclose who wrote them, and site health fixes.

- `ai-visibility-baseline`: How often ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity name you, counted across repeated answers, in a dated report.
- `get-cited-outreach`: Find the lists AI assistants cite, write to the person who edits each one, and check whether the page changed.
- `claim-chatgpt-business-profile`: Get ready for OpenAI's Business Hub: eligibility, verification, every profile field, then a check of what ChatGPT says.
- `reddit-quora-answers`: Replies that answer the question first and say you work on the product. You post them yourself.
- `fix-site-health`: Blocked crawlers, text that needs JavaScript, noindex and canonical mistakes: found, fixed on a branch, checked again.

Installation and details: https://github.com/rashempandit/apexmention-skills#readme, or https://apexmention.com/skills
